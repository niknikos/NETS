## What the programme is

The EAF-Nansen Programme supports partner countries and regional organisations in applying
an ecosystem approach to fisheries management. It is implemented by FAO with funding from
Norway, in close collaboration with the Institute of Marine Research (IMR) in Bergen, which
operates the research vessel *Dr. Fridtjof Nansen*. Survey work is planned with partner
countries and regional bodies and is increasingly ecosystem-wide, covering fish resources,
oceanography, plankton, habitats, pollution and climate-related questions.

## Survey types and what they produce

| Survey type | Typical outputs | Typical use by regional bodies |
|---|---|---|
| Acoustic (small pelagics, mesopelagics) | Biomass/abundance by species or group and region; length distributions; distribution maps | Indices for assessment working groups; distribution and shared-stock questions |
| Demersal swept-area trawl | Catch rates, swept-area biomass by species/group and depth stratum; length distributions | Indices; community indicators; bycatch and biodiversity |
| Ecosystem / multidisciplinary | Hydrography, oxygen, nutrients, plankton, habitat mapping, pollution (e.g. microplastics), top predators | Ecosystem-state reporting; environmental drivers; VME and habitat management |
| Seamount / high-seas | Species inventories, VME indicator taxa, deep-water fish | RFMO scientific committees (VME management, bottom-fishing footprints) |

## Comparability: the question every body will ask

Regional bodies mostly want to know whether new results can be read alongside earlier
ones. Changes that affect this include:

- the vessel (the programme has had successive vessels named *Dr. Fridtjof Nansen*);
- acoustic equipment and frequencies, calibration, and the target-strength relationships used;
- trawl gear, rigging, tow duration and the swept-area assumptions (catchability, effective width);
- survey timing relative to seasonal migrations, and spatial coverage (areas not surveyed);
- species identification and grouping (e.g. species reported as a group in earlier years);
- estimation software and method (e.g. a move to StoX, bootstrap-based uncertainty).

A synthesis should state which of these apply and what they mean for the body's use of the
results, even when the answer is "unknown". See [`evidence-standards.md`](evidence-standards.md).
