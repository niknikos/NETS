## Mandate and area

CECAF is an FAO regional fishery body with an advisory mandate. Its area is the Eastern
Central Atlantic, broadly from the Strait of Gibraltar southwards along West Africa (FAO
Major Fishing Area 34). Its **Scientific Sub-Committee (SSC)** reviews stock status and
formulates advice, drawing on **working groups** that assess stocks by resource type and
sub-region, notably small pelagic fish and demersal resources, each with northern and
southern subgroups. Working group reports are published by FAO.

## What it typically needs from a survey

- **Abundance/biomass indices** for assessed stocks (e.g. sardinellas, sardine, horse
  mackerels, anchovy and bonga for small pelagics; hakes, sparids, cephalopods, shrimps and
  others for demersals), by country or sub-region, with precision where available.
- **Length (and age) distributions** for recruitment signals and length-based methods.
- **Distribution and shared-stock information**: where a stock was found relative to
  national boundaries, which bears on joint assessment of shared stocks.
- **Coverage and comparability** with earlier survey series, since indices are used as
  tuning series in assessment models.
- Environmental context (upwelling, temperature, oxygen) that helps interpret changes.

## Synthesis profile

- **Purpose:** name the working group, the stocks and the assessment the results can
  inform.
- **What the survey found:** per stock or species group and area: index value, precision,
  length distribution summary; effort and coverage per area.
- **Limitations and comparability:** the comparability checklist is central here. State
  plainly whether the value can extend an existing index series and what may have changed.
- **Implications:** whether results support, question or cannot speak to current
  assessments; data gaps a future survey could fill.
- **Points for consideration:** e.g. how to treat a series break, requests for guidance
  on survey priorities.

## Sensitivity notes

- Multi-country surveys are the norm: clearance must be recorded for **each** country, and
  a sub-regional total is uncleared until all its areas are cleared.
- Shared-stock results can be sensitive during allocation discussions; flag to the user.
- Working group reports become public FAO documents.
