## Mandate and area

SWIOFC is an FAO regional fishery body with an advisory mandate for the **South West
Indian Ocean**, covering coastal states of East Africa and the western Indian Ocean island
states. Its **Scientific Committee** reviews the state of resources, often through working
groups or working parties on particular fisheries (e.g. shallow-water shrimp, demersal and
small pelagic resources; structure to verify). Many stocks are data-poor, so survey results
can be a principal source of information.

## What it typically needs from a survey

- **Resource status information** for stocks and species groups by country: biomass or
  catch-rate indices, length distributions, species composition.
- **Shared and straddling stocks** across the region's many EEZs.
- **Ecosystem and habitat information** (e.g. coastal habitats, oceanography, productivity).
- Guidance on how survey information can complement fisheries data in data-poor settings.

## Synthesis profile

- **Purpose:** the Scientific Committee or working party item, and which stocks.
- **What the survey found:** by country where cleared; species composition and indices.
- **Limitations:** single-survey snapshots, seasonal timing, gear selectivity for the
  species of interest; what is and is not comparable with national surveys.
- **Implications:** use in data-poor assessments; priorities for future monitoring.

## Sensitivity notes

- Many data owners: clearance per country; regional totals need all constituent areas cleared.
- Check whether the Scientific Committee's reports and working documents are public.
