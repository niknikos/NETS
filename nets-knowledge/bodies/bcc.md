## Mandate and area

The Benguela Current Convention, between **Angola, Namibia and South Africa**, established
the Benguela Current Commission to promote coordinated, ecosystem-based management of the
**Benguela Current Large Marine Ecosystem**. Its work spans fisheries, environmental
variability, pollution and biodiversity, with a science programme and advisory structures
(names to verify). It is an ecosystem-wide body rather than a stock-assessment RFMO.

## What it typically needs from a survey

- **Transboundary** information: distribution of shared stocks across national boundaries
  (e.g. horse mackerel, sardinella, hake), and changes in distribution.
- **Ecosystem state**: temperature, oxygen (including low-oxygen events), upwelling,
  plankton, harmful algal blooms, pollution, top predators.
- Results that feed regional state-of-the-ecosystem reporting and indicators.

## Synthesis profile

- **Purpose:** the BCC programme, report or indicator the results contribute to.
- **What the survey found:** ecosystem-wide, organised by theme; stock results by country
  where cleared, with emphasis on transboundary patterns.
- **Interpretation:** environmental drivers are often the core question; be explicit about
  correlation versus causation and about the single-survey snapshot.
- **Implications:** monitoring needs, coordination between the three countries' surveys.

## Sensitivity notes

- Three data owners: clearance per country. Transboundary maps or totals require all
  countries covered to have cleared them.
- Shared-stock distribution can be politically sensitive; flag to the user.
- Surveys may combine data from national vessels; their owners' terms apply too.
