## Mandate and area

SIOFA is an RFMO for the **high seas of the southern Indian Ocean**, covering fishery
resources other than tunas (which fall under IOTC). Its **Scientific Committee** advises
the Meeting of the Parties, supported by working groups on stock assessment and on
protected areas and ecosystems (names and structure to verify). Bottom fisheries on
seamounts and ridges, deep-water stocks and **VMEs** are central topics.

## What it typically needs from a survey

- Results from seamounts, ridges and banks in the high seas: species composition, deep-water
  fish (e.g. orange roughy, alfonsino, toothfish), size structure.
- **VME indicator taxa** and habitat information relevant to protected areas and
  bottom-fishing impact assessments.
- Biodiversity and ecosystem observations; methods and effort.

## Synthesis profile

- **Purpose:** the Scientific Committee or working group item addressed.
- **What the survey found:** by feature, at an agreed spatial resolution; VME encounters.
- **Limitations:** effort per feature, gear, depth coverage, identification level.
- **Implications:** protected-area proposals, bottom-fishing footprint and impact
  assessment, stock information for data-poor species.

## Sensitivity notes

- As for SEAFO: agree the spatial resolution of VME and aggregation information first.
- Features may lie close to national jurisdictions or extended continental shelf claims; if
  a feature straddles or adjoins a national zone, confirm clearance with the relevant state.
- Check the body's rules on confidentiality of data and documents.
